# Fotogramma 2.11.3

## Fixed

- **Sizes of 4 GiB and more were shown as 4.0 GB.** While scanning, the size of a file went through a 32-bit number, so every file of 4 GiB or more was listed as exactly 4.0 GB, with a bitrate computed from that. The wrong value also reached the totals ("GB analyzed", "GB to process"), the sort by size, the CSV and JSON exports and the quality advice (a 23 Mb/s remux was told it was "already well compressed"). Sizes are now 64-bit and correct. The conversion itself always read the real size from the disk, so its summary ("from 18.4 GB to 4.5 GB") was right.
- **The mascot stays on top of the bar.** She sometimes fell behind the head of the progress bar; her centre is now always on it, and she still stops short of the TV until the conversion is over.

## New

- **The profile advice is in Settings too.** Under the default profile, Settings describes it and has the **Which profile to choose?** comparison, as the conversion window does.
- **README and notes brought up to date**, with new English screenshots made from a demo library.

## Notes

- Your scan cache is dropped once, because its entries held the wrong sizes: the first scan after the update reads the files again, then it is fast as before.
