# Fotogramma 2.10.0

## New

- **Conversions in the background.** The progress window has a **Minimize to the background** button: it turns into a bar at the bottom with the progress, **Pause**, **Stop** and **Show**. While it runs the app stays usable: choose more videos and **Add to the queue**. Each batch keeps its own settings and starts when the previous one ends, never in parallel. Stop ends the whole queue, a video that is running or queued cannot be chosen twice, and a scan stays off while a conversion runs. Sleep or shutdown at the end happens after the last batch.
- **Closing says what is running.** The confirmation now talks about the conversion (before it always said "scan"), and warns that queued batches will be lost.

## Changed

- **Softer outlines.** Dropdown lists and popups have a soft purple outline instead of a coral one.

## Notes

- Only the batch that is running can be resumed after a crash or a forced close; batches waiting behind it are not saved.
